# LO-FI DREAD - Free PSX Horror Sampler

A free taste of the Chucky Assets PSX / PS1-style horror packs: 15 low-poly models, 5 inventory icons, 6 textures and the
PSX shader (vertex snapping, affine warping, dithered post effect). The download is a ready Godot 4 project with a small
walkable gallery; the GLB and OBJ files also work in Unity, Unreal and Blender.

Open the folder in Godot 4.4+ and press F5. WASD move, mouse look, Shift run, F flashlight, Esc frees the mouse.

## Folders
- `models/<pack>/<name>/` - `<name>.glb` (textures embedded), `<name>.obj` + `.mtl`, PNG textures
- `textures/psx_horror_textures/` - 128 px PS1 textures (tiling, decal with hard alpha)
- `icons/last_resort/` - 64 px inventory icons
- `addons/lofi_psx/` - PSX shader, post effect and `PsxApply` helper (MIT-style use inside your projects)
- `demo/sampler_gallery.tscn` - the gallery scene
